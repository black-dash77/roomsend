## Description

```text
RoomSend sends photos, videos, files and text to the devices on your Wi-Fi. Open it and the room fills in: every device running RoomSend or another app compatible with LocalSend shows up with its name and its own colored avatar.

Tap a name, choose photos, files, a few words or what you just copied, and watch it go. Send to one device or to the whole room at once, follow the progress in megabytes, and stop at any moment.

What arrives waits for you. You see who is sending and what, then you accept or decline. Photos and videos go to your library, other files to the Files app, and a message opens in full with a Copy button. If you prefer, turn on automatic acceptance, or ask senders for a PIN first.

Transfers are encrypted with HTTPS. Each device has its own certificate, and RoomSend checks that the device it talks to presents the fingerprint it announced. A device that does not encrypt is marked as such before you send anything.

The other person does not have the app? Share by link. RoomSend shows a QR code that opens a download page in any browser on the same Wi-Fi, for as long as RoomSend stays open.

Some networks block the usual search. RoomSend then asks the network address by address, and you can add a device by its IP address. Your own address is shown in the same window, so two people can read each other's. Mark the devices you use often as favorites to keep them at the top.

From Photos or any other app, tap Share and pick RoomSend. What you shared waits in RoomSend until you choose who gets it.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
RoomSend has no account and no server. Everything goes straight from one device to the other over your local network. No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Your history, your favorites and your name stay on your iPhone.

GOOD TO KNOW
RoomSend sends and receives while it is open on screen. Both devices must be on the same Wi-Fi network. RoomSend speaks version 2.2 of the LocalSend protocol.

You get a random name and avatar, which you can change at any time. Light or dark, the app follows your iPhone or your choice, in English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Portuguese, and you can switch language inside the app.
```
