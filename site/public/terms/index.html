The service

RoomSend is a free iPhone app that sends photos, files and text to the devices on the same local network. It follows the public LocalSend protocol and works with the LocalSend apps for Android, Windows, macOS and Linux. It has no account, no subscription, no in-app purchase and no ads.

Your use

You are responsible for what you send and for the devices you send it to. Only send what you have the right to share. Do not use RoomSend to pass on unlawful content, bother someone or reach a device without its owner's consent.

On a network you do not know, rather turn off "Visible on this Wi-Fi" and "Accept automatically".

Network and security

RoomSend depends on your local network: a network that isolates devices, or a firewall, can block discovery or transfers. Transfers are encrypted by default. Sharing by link uses plain HTTP and can be read by people on the same network.

A transfer can fail or be interrupted: keep a copy of your important files.
