Help

The questions RoomSend raises most often. For anything else, write to us.

Nobody in the room?

  1. Are both devices on the same Wi-Fi?

    Connect them to the same network. A guest network counts as another network.

  2. Is RoomSend or LocalSend open on screen on the other device?

    Open it. Devices only answer while the app is in front.

  3. Is RoomSend allowed on the local network?

    On the iPhone, open Settings, Privacy & Security, Local Network, and turn on RoomSend.

  4. Some networks keep devices apart, in hotels, offices or schools. Keep Scan the network on in RoomSend's Settings, or add the device by its address with the + button of the Room screen.

Questions

Where do received files go?

In the Files app, under On My iPhone, RoomSend. Photos and videos also go to Photos when Photos to Photos is on.

Which apps can I send to?

LocalSend on Android, Windows, macOS, Linux and iOS, and RoomSend on other iPhones, as long as they are on the same Wi-Fi.

The other device asks for a PIN.

Type the PIN shown on that device. To ask for one yourself: Settings, Ask for a PIN.

Are transfers encrypted?

Yes, by default: HTTPS, and RoomSend checks the other device's certificate against the fingerprint it announced. An open padlock means that device does not encrypt. Sharing by link uses plain HTTP.

How does sharing by link work?

In the send sheet, Share by link shows an address and a QR code. Any browser on the same Wi-Fi opens it and downloads the files, as long as RoomSend stays open.

Can I send to several devices at once?

Yes. In the carousel of the send sheet, pick The whole room.

How do I change my device's name?

In Settings, tap the name to type a new one, or tap the shuffle button to draw one.
